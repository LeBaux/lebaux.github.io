Mathematical Derivation: Clean 2D Projection Collapse

In special computational relativity, a 5D hypercube (penteract) consists of $2^5 = 32$ vertices defined by coordinate vectors $\mathbf{v} \in \{-1, 1\}^5$. To collapse 2 spatial dimensions cleanly onto a 3D subspace, we construct the parameter-dependent reduction operator $\hat{\pi}_{5 \to 3}(\gamma)$:

$$\mathbf{x}' = \begin{pmatrix} x_1 \\ x_2 \\ x_3 \end{pmatrix} + \gamma \cdot \mathbf{R}_{45}(\theta_{45}) \begin{pmatrix} x_4 \\ x_5 \end{pmatrix}, \quad \gamma \in [0, 1]$$
  • When $\gamma = 1$ (Full 5D Penteract): All 32 vertices and 80 hyper-edges span independent spatial dimensions with 10 $SO(5)$ rotation generators.
  • When $1 > \gamma > 0$ (Phase Folding): The 4th and 5th coordinates rotate around the $\theta_{45}$ plane while shrinking their metric scale, rendering 4D tesseracts folding inside the 3D projection window.
  • When $\gamma = 0$ (Clean 3D Collapse): $x_4$ and $x_5$ vanish completely. The 32 5D hyper-vertices map directly onto the $2^3 = 8$ vertices of a rigid 3D cube. Exactly 4 hyper-vertices coalesce at each of the 8 3D corners, multiplying localized field energy density $\rho_m$ by a factor of 4.
