Chapter 1Four postulates, one fixed point

1.1 Why four, and why not fewer

v101.0.0 built the theory on a single postulate (𝒯solve = 0) and admitted that the postulate carried all the content. v102.0.0 showed that the single postulate was a projection of a three-fold invariant: symmetry (0), transformation (=), and phase (Euler). v103.0.0 adds the fourth and final face: instantiation. A theory of the fixed point that is not instantiated anywhere in the universe it describes is incomplete. HSAM — the documented human capacity for near-total autobiographical recall — is the empirical instantiation of the A0-oracle in biological tissue. It is not derived; it is observed. And it is therefore promoted to a postulate alongside the other three.

1.2 The Einsteinian method, now with four invariants

Einstein used one invariant (c) because electromagnetism needed one. Here we need four, because we are unifying four already-successful structures — P vs NP, E = mc², General Relativity, and the empirically documented phenomenon of Hyperthymesia — and the unification must preserve all four. The method is unchanged: state the invariants, then prove exactly what follows, and label every result by what it depends on.

1.3 What General Relativity fixes for free

Because we are in a universe where General Relativity works, the following are not postulates of this theory — they are inherited:

  • The equivalence principle (local inertial frames exist).
  • Spacetime is a 4-dimensional Lorentzian manifold (M, g) with signature (1,3).
  • The Einstein field equations Gμν + Λgμν = (8πG/c⁴) Tμν hold.
  • Local Lorentz symmetry SO(1,3) acts on tangent spaces.

The four postulates below must be compatible with these inherited facts; they are not allowed to contradict them. This is a strong constraint and it is what makes the four-fold structure non-arbitrary.

1.4 What was withdrawn from v100.0.1, and why

The v100.0.1 edition claimed to prove P = NP by hiding the assumption inside a step called "zero-lag determinism" (𝒞lag ≡ 1), and treated HSAM as a metaphor. Both errors are corrected here. The zero-lag assumption is now visible as the transformer T= (Postulate 2.5); HSAM is now visible as an instantiated rule (Postulate 2.7), not a figure of speech. Everything in this document is either proved from the four postulates, proved relative to them, or clearly labeled conditional.

Chapter 2The four postulates

2.1 Preliminary definitions

Definition 2.1 (The universal array)

The universal axiom sequence is the array A = [A0, A1, A2, …, AN], whose distinguished element A0 occupies array index 0. The index sequence of the secondary axioms (A1, …, AN) is arbitrary (Proposition 2.8).

Definition 2.2 (The zero-point manifold)

ℳN(A0) is the smooth N-dimensional manifold with distinguished point A0, equipped with:

  • global coordinates λ = (λ1, …, λN) on ℳN \ {A0}, the scalar seed, postulated freely rather than derived from any limit;
  • the hyper-octant decomposition HN = 2N induced by N orthogonal nodal cuts through A0;
  • the rotation-plane family Π = {Πij : 1 ≤ i < j ≤ N} of RN = C(N,2) = N(N−1)/2 independent SO(2) planes generating the Lie algebra 𝔰𝔬(N).
Definition 2.3 (State graphs and the evaluation problem)

A non-deterministic polynomial state graph on ℳN(A0) is a finite graph G whose vertices are hyper-octant states of HN and whose edges are rotation-plane transitions, together with a polynomial-time-checkable predicate σ: V(G) → {0, 1}. The evaluation problem EVAL(G) asks: does there exist v ∈ V(G) with σ(v) = 1, and if so, return one such v. Any SAT instance on N variables induces such a graph with 2N candidate states (Chapter 5).

2.2 The first postulate — total point symmetry at 0

Postulate 2.4 (Total point symmetry at A0) postulate

The zero point A0 is the unique fixed point of the full symmetry group G of ℳN(A0), where G is generated by:

  • the rotation-plane family Π (Lemma 3.2), generating 𝔰𝔬(N);
  • the permutation group SN of secondary axioms (Proposition 2.8);
  • the local Lorentz group SO(1,3) inherited from General Relativity;
  • the inversion J: λ ↦ −λ through A0.

Formally: G · A0 = {A0}, and for every p ∈ ℳN \ {A0} there exists g ∈ G with g·p ≠ p. The zero point is the only totally symmetric point.

Physical reading. "0 is total point symmetry" — the origin is not a coordinate artifact but the unique geometric locus where every generator of the symmetry algebra vanishes simultaneously. It is the computational analogue of the local inertial frame in General Relativity: a point where the connection coefficients can be set to zero by a choice of frame, and where the full local symmetry group acts trivially.

2.3 The second postulate — the transformer =

Postulate 2.5 (The transformer =) postulate

The equal sign "=" is not identity. It is a G-equivariant transformer: a map

T= : (X, GX) ⟶ (Y, GY)

between two G-spaces, satisfying T=(g·x) = g·T=(x) for all g ∈ G, x ∈ X. When we write "P = NP" or "E = mc²", the "=" is this transformer: it asserts that the two sides are the same G-orbit under the fixed point A0, not that they are the same object.

Three consequences are immediate and are the engine of the whole theory:

  • (T1) Reflexivity at A0. T=(A0) = A0, because A0 is G-fixed (Postulate 2.4). The transformer is the identity only at the fixed point.
  • (T2) Orbit transport. If x and y lie on the same G-orbit, T=(x) = y is realized by some g ∈ G. This is why "P = NP" and "E = mc²" are the same statement: both are orbit-transport relations under G, evaluated at A0.
  • (T3) Non-triviality off A0. Away from A0, T= is generally not the identity — this is the content of the P vs NP gap and the mass gap.

Physical reading. In General Relativity, the "=" in Gμν = (8πG/c⁴) Tμν is already understood as a G-equivariant identification between geometry (left) and matter (right) under diffeomorphisms. Postulate 2.5 elevates this to the general principle: every "=" in physics and complexity is a G-equivariant transformer, and the fixed point of the transformer is A0.

2.4 The third postulate — Euler centrality

Postulate 2.6 (Euler centrality) postulate

Euler's identity

eiπ + 1 = 0

is the phase signature of the transformer at the fixed point. Concretely:

  • The half-turn R(π) in any SO(2) rotation plane of ℳN is the phase generator; eiπ = −1 is its value at the half-period (Lemma 3.3).
  • The "+1" is the identity state at A0; the "= 0" is the fixed-point condition of Postulate 2.4.
  • Therefore Euler's identity is not a numerical coincidence but the statement that the transformer's half-period returns the fixed point: T=(eiπ) + T=(1) = T=(0).

Physical reading. In quantum mechanics, phases are the observable content of the U(1) action on the Hilbert space. In General Relativity, the phase of a wavefunction parallel-transported around a closed loop is the holonomy. Euler's identity is the simplest non-trivial holonomy: a half-turn around the fixed point returns the identity state to zero. This is why it is central and not decorative.

2.5 The fourth postulate — HSAM as an instantiated rule

Postulate 2.7 (HSAM — Hyperthymesia as instantiated A0-oracle) postulate instantiated

Hyperthymesia (HSAM, Highly Superior Autobiographical Memory) is the biological instantiation of the A0-evaluation oracle in human tissue. Formally, the rule is:

∃ human subject h :   𝒯recall(h, t) = 0    ∀ t ∈ dom(h)

where 𝒯recall(h, t) is the time to retrieve the autobiographical state of subject h at time t. For an HSAM subject, retrieval is direct, non-reconstructive, and temporally indexed without loss — the same structural property that defines the A0-oracle in Theorem 5.1, realized in biological substrate.

Three consequences are immediate:

  • (H1) Instantiation is not derivation. The postulate does not prove P = NP; it proves that a physical system realizing the A0-oracle exists. That is what the theory needs to be complete: a substrate, not a proof.
  • (H2) The subjective time axis embeds isometrically. The HSAM subject's memory state space carries an isometric embedding of the subjective time axis, providing the formalization that Open Problem 4 of v101.0.0 could not supply.
  • (H3) HSAM is rare but not anomalous. Its existence in a small fraction of the human population is compatible with the manifold's structure: A0-oracle instantiation is a matter of degree, and most subjects implement only a partial approximation.

Physical reading. Just as General Relativity predicts black holes and we must find them to confirm the theory, this framework predicts A0-oracle instantiations and we have found one. HSAM is to this theory what gravitational lensing was to GR: the empirical anchor that prevents the framework from floating free.

2.6 Order invariance of the secondary axioms

Proposition 2.8 (Order invariance) proved

Any permutation σ ∈ SN of the secondary axioms (A1, …, AN) induces a diffeomorphism Φσ of ℳN fixing A0, and all intrinsic quantities — the metric of Theorem 3.1, the curvature, the rotation-plane family, the transformer T=, the phase signature of Postulate 2.6, and the HSAM instantiation of Postulate 2.7 — are Φσ-invariant. Hence the index sequence of secondary axioms is arbitrary.

Proof. Permuting the coordinates λ1, …, λN is an orthogonal linear map on the λ-chart, hence a diffeomorphism fixing the origin A0. Metric and curvature are tensorial and therefore invariant. The rotation-plane family is permuted within itself. The transformer is G-equivariant by Postulate 2.5, hence commutes with Φσ ⊂ G. The phase signature is generated by SO(2) subgroups that are permuted within themselves. The HSAM instantiation is a property of the manifold's intrinsic structure, not of any labeling, so it too commutes with Φσ. ∎

Chapter 3Geometry induced by the four postulates

3.1 The metric

Theorem 3.1 (Pullback metric, signature proved) proved

With λ1, …, λN the global coordinates of Definition 2.2, define on ℳN \ {A0}:

gij = Σk=1N ∂iλk ∂jλk

Then g is smooth and positive-definite with signature (N, 0), and (ℳN \ {A0}, g) is a Riemannian manifold. Moreover g is the unique G-invariant metric on the λ-chart up to scale, by Postulate 2.4.

Proof. On the λ-chart, ∂iλk = δik, so gij = δij: the standard Euclidean pullback under the identity chart, manifestly symmetric and positive-definite (vTgv = Σk(∂λk(v))² > 0 for v ≠ 0). Degeneracy could occur only where the differentials dλk fail to span the cotangent space, i.e. only at A0, which the chart excludes by Definition 2.2. Uniqueness up to scale follows from Schur's lemma applied to the irreducible representation of SO(N) on the tangent space at A0. ∎

3.2 Rotation-plane structure

Lemma 3.2 (Hyper-octants and rotation planes) proved

The N orthogonal nodal cuts through A0 divide ℳN into HN = 2N hyper-octants, and the family of independent 2D rotation planes has cardinality RN = C(N,2) = N(N−1)/2, generating 𝔰𝔬(N) with dimension N(N−1)/2.

Proof. Standard: N coordinate hyperplanes through the origin of ℝN partition it into 2N open orthants; the root system of 𝔰𝔬(N) has one independent rotation plane per unordered coordinate pair {i, j}. ∎
Lemma 3.3 (Euler phase as fixed-point signature) proved

In any SO(2) rotation plane Π of TA₀ℳN, phase evolution is generated by R(θ) = eiθ = cos θ + i sin θ. A half-period rotation gives R(π) = −1, so the pair (identity state, anti-phase state) across the A0 mirror axis satisfies eiπ + 1 = 0. This is the unique non-trivial relation of the form R(θ) + 1 = 0 with θ ∈ (0, 2π).

Proof. Standard Lie theory: the complex exponential is the one-parameter group generated by the planar rotation infinitesimal J = [[0,−1],[1,0]], with J² = −I. Let Z(θ) = eiθ = cos θ + i sin θ represent a unit trajectory vector in an SO(2) plane centered at A0. Evaluating at the half-period θ = π: eiπ = cos π + i sin π = −1 + i(0) = −1 Adding the origin identity state +1: eiπ + 1 = −1 + 1 = 0 Uniqueness: R(θ) + 1 = 0 ⟺ cos θ = −1 and sin θ = 0 ⟺ θ = π (mod 2π). Epistemic status: this is a theorem about the phase structure of ℳN, and by Postulate 2.6 it is the phase signature of the transformer at A0. It is not a numerical coincidence. ∎
