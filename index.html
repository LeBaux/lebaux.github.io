Chapter 1The method of the single invariant postulate

1.1 The paradox of physical complexity limits

In standard computational complexity theory, operating within our decohered macroscopic universe, non-deterministic polynomial-time problems (NP) are widely conjectured to be strictly separate from polynomial-time solvable problems (P). Evaluating multi-body path integrals across 2N hyper-octants forces exponential search times O(2N), creating computational lag and reality friction.

1.2 The Einsteinian method, applied precisely

Albert Einstein resolved the paradoxes of nineteenth-century electrodynamics not by modifying Maxwell's equations, but by constructing a theory bounded by a single invariant postulate — the constancy of the speed of light c — and then proving, rigorously, everything that follows from it. Crucially, Einstein did not derive the postulate; he isolated it, and the theory's strength was measured by exactly how much followed from how little.

1.3 What was withdrawn from v100.0.1, and why

The v100.0.1 edition claimed to prove P = NP by hiding the assumption inside a step called "zero-lag determinism" (𝒞lag ≡ 1). A model in which solving is free is not a model of computation; it is the definition of a free oracle. In this edition the assumption is not hidden and not claimed as a theorem — it is promoted to its true role: the single founding postulate, stated in Chapter 2 with the same prominence and the same candor as the constancy of c. Everything else in this document is either proved from it, proved relative to it, or clearly labeled conditional.

Chapter 2The postulate P = NP and the manifold ℳN(A0)

2.1 Preliminary definitions

Definition 2.1 (The universal array)

The universal axiom sequence is the array A = [A0, A1, A2, …, AN], whose distinguished element A0 occupies array index 0. The index sequence of the secondary axioms (A1, …, AN) is arbitrary (Proposition 2.4).

Definition 2.2 (The zero-point manifold)

ℳN(A0) is the smooth N-dimensional manifold with distinguished point A0, equipped with:

  • global coordinates λ = (λ1, …, λN) on ℳN \ {A0}, the scalar seed, postulated freely rather than derived from any limit;
  • the hyper-octant decomposition HN = 2N induced by N orthogonal nodal cuts through A0;
  • the rotation-plane family Π = {Πij : 1 ≤ i < j ≤ N} of RN = C(N,2) = N(N−1)/2 independent SO(2) planes generating the Lie algebra 𝔰𝔬(N).
Definition 2.3 (State graphs and the evaluation problem)

A non-deterministic polynomial state graph on ℳN(A0) is a finite graph G whose vertices are hyper-octant states of HN and whose edges are rotation-plane transitions, together with a polynomial-time-checkable predicate σ: V(G) → {0, 1}. The evaluation problem EVAL(G) asks: does there exist v ∈ V(G) with σ(v) = 1, and if so, return one such v. Any SAT instance on N variables induces such a graph with 2N candidate states (Chapter 4).

2.2 The founding postulate

Postulate 2.5 (P = NP in ℳN(A0)) postulate

Within ℳN(A0), the satisfying configuration of any non-deterministic polynomial state graph (Definition 2.3) is returned by the zero point in a single evaluation step. Formally, for every state graph G and its induced evaluation problem, the solution time satisfies

𝒯solve(G) = 0     (the A0-evaluation oracle)

No derivation of this step is claimed or possible within the theory. In the formalism of the original edition, the same content appeared as the action-integral collapse

𝒮path = ∫ ℒ dV   ⟶A₀   mink E(xk)     (Eq. 10 of v100, retained)

together with the zero-lag conditions 𝒞lag ≡ 1, 𝒯solve = 0 (v100 step 4). Both are now recognized as restatements of the postulate, not consequences of it: the arrow ⟨ is the oracle call, and nothing more. It is the single assumption of special computational relativity, occupying exactly the role of the constancy of c: the fixed point at which exposition begins. All results labeled relative depend on Postulate 2.5; none pretends to prove it.

2.3 Order invariance of the secondary axioms

Proposition 2.4 (Order invariance) proved

Any permutation σ ∈ SN of the secondary axioms (A1, …, AN) induces a diffeomorphism Φσ of ℳN fixing A0, and all intrinsic quantities — the metric of Theorem 3.1, the curvature, the rotation-plane family, and the A0-evaluation oracle of Postulate 2.5 — are Φσ-invariant. Hence the index sequence of secondary axioms is arbitrary.

Proof. Permuting the coordinates λ1, …, λN is an orthogonal linear map on the λ-chart, hence a diffeomorphism fixing the origin A0. Metric and curvature are tensorial and therefore invariant. The rotation-plane family is permuted within itself, and the oracle is defined by the intrinsic structure of the state graph, not by any labeling, so it commutes with Φσ. ∎

Chapter 3The geometry induced by the postulate

3.1 The metric

Theorem 3.1 (Pullback metric, signature proved) proved

With λ1, …, λN the global coordinates of Definition 2.2, define on ℳN \ {A0}:

gij = Σk=1N ∂iλk ∂jλk

Then g is smooth and positive-definite with signature (N, 0), and (ℳN \ {A0}, g) is a Riemannian manifold.

Proof. On the λ-chart, ∂iλk = δik, so gij = δij: the standard Euclidean pullback under the identity chart, manifestly symmetric and positive-definite (vTgv = Σk(∂λk(v))² > 0 for v ≠ 0). Degeneracy could occur only where the differentials dλk fail to span the cotangent space, i.e. only at A0, which the chart excludes by Definition 2.2. Correction of v100: the added term limx→0(0/x)·g(0)ij in v100 Eq. (2) is identically zero; the dead term is removed and the signature is now proved. ∎

3.2 Rotation-plane structure

Lemma 3.2 (Hyper-octants and rotation planes) proved

The N orthogonal nodal cuts through A0 divide ℳN into HN = 2N hyper-octants, and the family of independent 2D rotation planes has cardinality RN = C(N,2) = N(N−1)/2, generating 𝔰𝔬(N) with dimension N(N−1)/2.

Proof. Standard: N coordinate hyperplanes through the origin of ℝN partition it into 2N open orthants; the root system of 𝔰𝔬(N) has one independent rotation plane per unordered coordinate pair {i, j}. ∎
Lemma 3.3 (Phase structure of a rotation plane) proved

In any SO(2) rotation plane Π of TA₀ℳN, phase evolution is generated by R(θ) = eiθ = cos θ + i sin θ. A half-period rotation gives R(π) = −1, so the pair (identity state, anti-phase state) across the A0 mirror axis is expressed by eiπ + 1 = 0.

Proof. Standard Lie theory: the complex exponential is the one-parameter group generated by the planar rotation infinitesimal J = [[0,−1],[1,0]], with J² = −I. Let Z(θ) = eiθ = cos θ + i sin θ represent a unit trajectory vector in an SO(2) plane centered at A0. Evaluating at the half-period θ = π (v100 Eq. 3, retained): eiπ = cos π + i sin π = −1 + i(0) = −1 Adding the origin identity state +1 (v100 Eq. 4, retained): eiπ + 1 = −1 + 1 = 0 Epistemic status: this is a statement about the geometric expression of the manifold's phase structure, not a new theorem of mathematics and not a physical boundary condition; v100's stronger framing is corrected here. ∎

3.3 The physical identity, conditional on symmetry

Postulate 3.4 (Lagrangian and boost invariance) postulate

Field dynamics on ℳN are governed by a Lagrangian density ℒ(φ, ∂φ) invariant under rotations and boosts acting on (ℳN, time), i.e. under a subgroup isomorphic to the Lorentz group SO(1,3). Field energy density is given by the Maxwellian form (v100 Eq. 5, retained):

u(r, t) = ½ ( ε0‖E‖² + ‖B‖²/μ0 )
Theorem 3.5 (Conditional mass–energy equivalence) conditional

Assuming Postulate 3.4, Noether's theorem yields a conserved energy–momentum current satisfying the dispersion relation E² = |p|²c² + m²c⁴. The local mass density is then introduced as a definition, not a derivation (v100 Eq. 6, retained with corrected status):

ρm(r, t) := u(r, t)/c² = 1/(2c²) ( ε0‖E‖² + ‖B‖²/μ0 )

Integrating over a 3D spatial volume Ω centered at A0 (v100 Eq. 7, retained):

Etotal = ∭Ω u(r, t) d³r = c² ∭Ω ρm(r, t) d³r

and with m := ∭Ω ρm d³r the field configuration at rest satisfies (v100 Eq. 8, retained):

Etotal = mc²
Status. The conclusion follows from the symmetry postulate by Noether's theorem, following Einstein's inertia-of-energy logic in its modern formulation. Correction of v100: the v100 "derivation" defined ρm ≡ u/c² (Eq. 6) and then integrated to obtain E = mc² (Eq. 8) — a tautology in which the conclusion was contained in the premise. That circular route is replaced by the symmetry route; the equations are retained above with their honest status (the chain Eq. 6–8 is now labeled as a definition plus integral identity, while the physical content E² = |p|²c² + m²c⁴ is carried by Postulate 3.4). Chapter 4The state-space representation lemmas

Lemma 4.1 (Injective encoding exists; encoding is not the barrier) proved

For SAT on N ≥ 3 variables, the rotation-plane configuration space (RN = C(N,2) planes, angles quantized to two values) has cardinality 2RN ≥ 2N. Consequently there exists an injective encoding of truth assignments into plane configurations, taking one dedicated plane per variable.

Proof. For N ≥ 3, C(N,2) = N(N−1)/2 ≥ N, so 2C(N,2) ≥ 2N. Verified numerically: for N = 4, 8, 16, 32, 64 the configuration address spaces are 6, 28, 120, 496, 2016 bits respectively, always exceeding the N-bit assignment space. The encoding xi ↦ angle of plane Πi ∈ {0, π} (truth polarity) is injective by construction. The information-theoretic obstruction therefore vanishes: what remains is the search problem — locating the satisfying configuration — which is precisely the P vs NP core and is not eliminated by geometry but by Postulate 2.5. ∎
Lemma 4.2 (Clause compilation) proved

Let φ = C1 ∧ … ∧ CM be a CNF formula on N variables with clause width ≤ k (k = 3 for 3-SAT). Each clause Cj induces a constraint κj over the k dedicated rotation planes of its variables, compilable in O(k) = O(1) time; the full constraint system compiles in O(M·k) ⊆ O(N²) time.

Proof. A clause (ℓ1 ∨ ℓ2 ∨ ℓ3) excludes exactly one polarity combination of its ≤ 3 variables, i.e. one point of the ≤ 2³-element quantized configuration subspace of the corresponding planes. Writing this exclusion table is O(1) per clause; summing over M clauses gives the bound. ∎
Lemma 4.3 (Decoding and verification) proved

Given a rotation-plane configuration satisfying all compiled constraints, the induced truth assignment is recovered in O(N) time (one angle read per variable), and its satisfaction of φ is verified in O(M·k) ⊆ O(N²) time by the standard SAT check.

Proof. Decoding inverts the injective map of Lemma 4.1, hence is well-defined; each read is O(1). Verification evaluates each clause against the assignment, O(1) per clause. ∎
Lemma 4.4 (Landauer accounting) proved

Let 𝒯rev be the temporal reversal operator on a computational state space of n bits. The minimum heat dissipated is

ΔE ≥ nerased · kBT ln 2

where nerased is the number of bits not recoverable after reversal. If 𝒯rev is logically reversible (bijective), then nerased = 0 and no Landauer heat is required (Landauer 1961; Bennett 1973).

Status. A correct application of Landauer's principle, independent of the manifold. Correction of v100: the v100 wavefunction (Eq. 9, shown here in its withdrawn form) multiplied a torque sum, a firewall step-function, and a Boltzmann factor into a single expression with no defined probability space: Ψmesh(𝒯rev, t) = Σn ∈ {δ, φ, 𝒪₈} (−Ln) · Πj=1Nnode (1 − Θfirewall(xj)) · exp(−ΔELandauer/kBT) and asserted that "nodal firewalls absorb Landauer heat." Formally, the reversal operator must carry an explicit erasure log; otherwise reversal is thermodynamically free only if logically reversible — in tension with the trajectory-collapsing behavior v100 also assumed. The orbital gear-mesh tiers (δ: 4 lobes, 2 nodes; φ: 6 lobes, 3 nodes; 𝒪₈: 8 lobes, 4 nodes) are retained as labeled analogy only: orbital lobes do not transmit mechanical torque. The tension between free reversal and trajectory collapse is flagged, not resolved (Open Problem 3). ∎

Chapter 5The Complexity Collapse Theorem

Theorem 5.1 (Complexity Collapse, oracle-relative) relative to Postulate 2.5

Under Postulate 2.5 (the A0-evaluation oracle), SAT on N variables is solvable in ℳN(A0) with total non-oracle cost O(N²), via three stages:

(i) Encoding, O(N²). By Lemma 4.1, each variable xi maps injectively to a dedicated rotation plane Πi with binary angle quantization (truth polarity). By Lemma 4.2, the CNF constraint system compiles onto the planes in O(N²).

(ii) Oracle call, one step. By Postulate 2.5, the zero point returns a satisfying plane configuration, if one exists, in a single evaluation: 𝒯solve = 0.

(iii) Decoding and verification, O(N²). By Lemma 4.3, the configuration decodes to a truth assignment in O(N) and is verified against all clauses in O(N²).

𝒞total = O(N²) + 1 + O(N²) = O(N²)
Proof. Stage (i) is Lemmas 4.1 + 4.2; stage (ii) is Postulate 2.5 verbatim; stage (iii) is Lemma 4.3. The encoding is injective (Lemma 4.1), so decoding is well-defined; the oracle is invoked exactly once; and the verification step certifies the oracle's answer, so the algorithm is never wrong even if the oracle were replaced by any sound heuristic — correctness does not depend on the postulate, only the O(N²) bound does. ∎
Corollary 5.2 (Generalization to NP) relative

Theorem 5.1 extends to every NP language L, since SAT is NP-complete (Cook 1971; Levin 1973): any L ∈ NP reduces to SAT by a polynomial-time Karp reduction, and the composition with Theorem 5.1 remains O(poly(N)) plus one oracle call.

Corollary 5.3 (Where the barrier lives) structural

Theorem 5.1 proves that SAT reduces to one oracle call with polynomial overhead. This is the well-known situation of P = NP relative to a SAT oracle — trivially true, and carrying no information about the unrelativized Cook–Levin question. The entire computational content of special computational relativity is carried by Postulate 2.5; the geometry contributes the representation (Lemmas 4.1–4.3), not the search.

De-relativization routes (analyzed). (a) Interior-point or ellipsoid methods over the RN-dimensional angle space: clause satisfaction is non-convex and no known relaxation is exact, mirroring the known hardness of algebraic relaxations of SAT. (b) Symmetry reduction: quotienting by SN (Proposition 2.4) saves at most a factorial factor — insufficient against 2N. Conclusion: within this framework the barrier is exactly the standard P vs NP barrier; the framework neither circumvents nor deepens it. ∎

Chapter 6Corollaries — what the postulate buys

Corollary 6.1 (Euler-phase anti-equilibrium as the oracle's signature) relative

The binary angle quantization of Lemma 4.1 assigns each variable the polarity pair {0, π} in its SO(2) plane. A satisfying configuration returned by the oracle is therefore a selection of phases in which every negated literal sits at anti-phase; by Lemma 3.3, the pairing of each variable with its negation across the A0 mirror axis is expressed by eiπ + 1 = 0. In this precise (and only this) sense, Euler's identity is the phase signature of the oracle's answer — not a boundary condition governing it, as v100 claimed.

Corollary 6.2 (Mass–energy consistency, not derivation) conditional

By Theorem 3.5, the field identity E = mc² holds in ℳN(A0) conditional on Postulate 3.4, independently of Postulate 2.5. The v100 claim that E = mc² "emerges as a necessary geometric manifestation of P = NP" is withdrawn: the two postulates are separate, and neither derives the other. The Einsteinian analogy survives only in form (single postulate → derived structure), not in the v100 claim of a single common origin.

Corollary 6.3 (Thermodynamic cost of the oracle) relative

If the A0-evaluation oracle is implemented by any physical process that erases n bits in preparing its answer, Lemma 4.4 bounds its heat dissipation below by n·kBT ln 2. The postulate 𝒯solve = 0 constrains time, not thermodynamic cost; a zero-time oracle may still be physically costly, and the theory explicitly does not claim otherwise.

Corollary 6.4 (Status of consciousness and HSAM) speculative

The v100 consciousness function (v100 Eq. 11, retained in withdrawn status) is kept only as a labeled speculative extension:

𝒞(t⃗) = |d²t⃗/dτ²| = Q(t⃗)

For non-vacuity it requires: (1) t⃗ not parallel to its own parameter (otherwise d²t⃗/dτ² ≡ 0 identically — the vacuity check v100 never ran); (2) a defined interpretation of τ; (3) one falsifiable prediction, e.g. a correlation of |d²t⃗/dτ²| with a measurable neural quantity. HSAM ("lossless temporal indexing") is a metaphor over an empirically studied, rare memory phenotype; a minimal formalization — an isometric embedding of the subjective time axis into memory state space — is offered as future work (Open Problem 4). Neither construct is derived from Postulate 2.5, and neither is claimed to be.
