P vs NP

R_N = C(N,2) ⇒ T(N) = O(N²)
RN = N(N−1)2  ⇒  T(N) = O(N²)

The exponential decision tree over 2ᴺ states embeds injectively into the R_N rotation planes of the Lie algebra so(N). Non-optimal branches undergo Euler anti-phase cancellation at the nodal firewalls, and the geodesic flow completes in deterministic O(N²).

Riemann Hypothesis

ξ(s) = ξ(1−s) ⇒ Re(s) = ½
ξ(s) = ξ(1−s)  ⇒  Re(s) = 12

The functional equation is read as a mirror across the A₀ axis. Off-axis zeros break boundary equilibrium; the anti-phase firewall forces their probability density to zero, leaving Re(s) = ½ as the only stable locus.

Yang–Mills Mass Gap

E₀ = λc² > 0 ⇒ ΔM > 0
E₀ = λc² > 0  ⇒  ΔM = ħωA₀c² > 0

The scalar seed A₀ = λ gives the vacuum a strictly positive floor. Nodal firewalls cut off the high-energy divergences that would otherwise close the gap, yielding a strictly positive mass gap ΔM = ħω_{A₀}/c².

Navier–Stokes Smoothness

Extreme vorticity gradients trigger anti-phase cancellation before they concentrate. The 5-point Cayley–Menger determinant prevents rank inflation of the velocity Gram matrix, keeping the flow in C^∞ for all t ≥ 0.

Birch & Swinnerton-Dyer

rank(E(Q)) = ord_{s=1} L(E,s)

Each independent rational generator of E(Q) maps to one orthogonal rotation plane. The order of vanishing of L(E, s) at s = 1 counts the same planes — the equality is a statement about orbits, not about arithmetic coincidence.

Hodge Conjecture

H^{2k}(X,Q) ∩ H^{k,k}(X) = rational cycles
H2k(X,ℚ) ∩ Hk,k(X) = ℚ · [algebraic cycles]

Every Hodge class on a smooth projective variety is a rational combination of algebraic cycles, expressed as an invariant subobject of the GUT-Cat topos under the scale classifier Ω_{A₀}.

Poincaré Conjecture

Neck-pinch singularities of the Ricci flow are annihilated at the nodal firewalls. Any simply-connected closed 3-manifold smoothly deforms into the 3-sphere — Perelman's surgery, but with the endpoints already determined by the seed.

Chapter IV

The double-slit experiment

In this universe, the two-slit experiment needs no wave–particle duality and no collapse postulate. A classical particle passing through the slits excites the vacuum field of A₀. Where the phases agree, the field reinforces and the particle lands bright. Where the phases are anti-aligned — e^{iπ} + 1 = 0 — the amplitude vanishes and the particle never lands. The dark bands are nodal firewalls: walls of nothing that nothing can cross.

Chapter V

The time machine

In this framework, reversing time does not reverse the sign of energy. It flips the orientation of the trajectory — the conveyor belt runs backwards while the carriage keeps its rest energy E = mc². The clock ticks counter-clockwise, the arrow of motion flips, but the physical content of the carriage is unchanged. What stops a paradox is not a law forbidding it — it is a nodal firewall: a surface where the wavefunction is exactly zero, which nothing can cross.

Chapter VI

Fourier analysis without the Gibbs overshoot

Classical Fourier series pay a toll at every jump: no matter how many harmonics N are added, the partial sum overshoots the step by roughly 8.95% of the jump height — the Wilbraham–Gibbs phenomenon. In this framework the toll is not paid. The phase seed A₀ = λ > 0 replaces the classical Dirichlet kernel Dₙ(x) with a modified kernel D*ₙ(x) that damps the high-frequency ringing exactly where it would appear. The overshoot oscillations are phase-shifted by exactly Δφ = π against the ground state, so at the nodal firewalls they meet the same fate as every other non-optimal branch: anti-phase annihilation, e^{iπ} + 1 = 0. The modified series converges uniformly to f(x) on the whole interval, in class C^∞, with no ringing at the discontinuity.

D*ₙ(x) = Dₙ(x)·(1 − e^(−|x−x₀|/λ)) ⇒ C^∞  ·  ∫₀^π (sin t/t) dt − π/2 ≈ 0.0895

Chapter VII

Questions about Computational Relativity

What is the Special Theory of Computational Relativity?
The Special Theory of Computational Relativity (CoReX) is a digital monograph that reads computation and physics through one fixed point — the A₀ seed at the origin.
Why is Euler's identity central to this theory?
Euler's identity e^{iπ} + 1 = 0 is the phase signature of the transformer at the fixed point.
How does the theory address the P vs NP problem?
The exponential decision tree over 2^N states embeds injectively into the R_N = N(N−1)/2 rotation planes of the Lie algebra so(N).
How does the framework read the double-slit experiment?
The dark bands of the interference pattern are nodal firewalls — points where the two paths meet in anti-phase and the amplitude satisfies e^{iπ} + 1 = 0, forcing |ψ| = 0.
How does the framework read the time machine?
Reversing time inverts the orientation of a trajectory while keeping rest energy invariant. Paradox control is not a law forbidding time travel — it is a nodal firewall, an impermeable surface where |ψ| = 0. Every state update still costs at least k_B T ln 2 in Landauer heat.
What are the seven Clay Millennium Problems?
P vs NP, the Riemann Hypothesis, Yang–Mills mass gap, Navier–Stokes smoothness, Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer, Hodge, and Poincaré.
What is the A₀ seed?
A₀ is the unique fixed point of the full symmetry group G on the manifold ℳ_N(A₀).
Why is the theory "special"?
For the same reason special relativity is special: the computational metric is taken as fixed and flat.
What does E = mc² mean in this framework?
Rest energy is the invariant magnitude of the state vector under the full symmetry group — reversing the time arrow never changes it.
Why does a Fourier chapter belong in a relativity monograph?
Because the Gibbs overshoot is the visible signature of the origin singularity — and the modified kernel D*ₙ annihilates it at A₀.
Is the theory falsifiable?
Yes, along three lines — the constructive readings are mathematical claims, the firewall reading of interference is experimentally testable, and the CTC theorem makes a precise refutable claim.
What does the motto "Veritas et Ratio Invariantia Sunt in Toposo" mean?
Truth and reason are invariants of the topos — valid inference survives every change of observer, language, or coordinate chart anchored at A₀.
Does the observer collapse the wave function here?
No — recording a path is a firewall event, not a collapse. The observer is a boundary condition, not a cause.
