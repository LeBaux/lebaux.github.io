Abstract

We present the formal dissertation establishing the special theory of computational relativity, proving that P = NP holds constructively within an idealized N-dimensional relativistic manifold ℳN(A0) anchored by a foundational zero-point singularity axiom (Axiom A0) at position 0 of the universal array A. We prove that the array ordering of secondary axioms (A1 … AN) is arbitrary, as all state spaces, physical properties, and rotation planes oscillate symmetrically around array position 0. Within this special universe, we prove that:

  1. Euler’s identity (eiπ + 1 = 0) governs anti-phase boundary oscillations across A0.
  2. Euclidean and SO(N) Lie algebra geometry emerges dynamically from scale parameter gradients.

  4. Chrono-structural time travel is physically realizable via zero-probability nodal firewalls (|ψnode| = 0) isolating past and future trajectories during momentum reversal (−Ln).

Finally, we establish the theorem of complexity collapse, proving that continuous path action integrals collapse into O(N2) polynomial-time algebraic minimizations in ℳN(A0), yielding P = NP.
