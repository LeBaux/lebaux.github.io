About this explainer

Values of ζ(s) are computed live in the browser using the Euler–Maclaurin formula. Zero ordinates are the standard published values (14.1347…, 21.0220…, 25.0109…, …). The Riemann Hypothesis remains open — nothing here proves or disproves it.
