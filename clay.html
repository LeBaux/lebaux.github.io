GRADE S/C · Structural analogy

Zeros as anti-phase equilibria

Each zero of the mirror zeta is a nodal surface (|ψnode| = 0) pinned to the A₀ mirror axis, the image of the critical line Re(s) = ½. With firewalls on, order invariance forces every zero onto the mirror. Turn firewalls off and the pinning postulate (P-zeta) is withdrawn — zeros drift off the line.

Honesty note

Mirror-pinning is derived in-manifold, but the identification of the classical Riemann zeta with the mirror zeta is an added postulate with no independent evidential force. A single off-line zero falsifies P-zeta (falsifier F-4).

Derived, not postulated

The gap is crystallized by colliding a positive integer property with its negative fractional inverse across A₀. Landauer firewalls double as an infrared cutoff: no gapless propagation channel exists. Missing: the 4D truncation theorem to Osterwalder–Schrader axioms (falsifier F-2).

Two dissipative floors

(i) Landauer floor: every erased bit costs kBT ln 2, so dissipation is bounded below. (ii) Nodal firewalls forbid measure-zero vorticity concentration. In-manifold: smooth for all time. Missing bridge: deriving Navier–Stokes as the mean field of tier dynamics.

GRADE S · Structural analogy

Crystallization as algebraicity

Positive integer properties Pₙ (right, blue) stream toward the A₀ mirror; negative fractional anti-properties (left, red) stream back. Each collision crystallizes a constant — δc, φc, O₈ — the framework's image of an algebraic cycle. Mirror-fixed (n,n) classes are exactly these collision products: a discrete lattice with rational structure constants.

Honesty note

No Kähler form, no projective embedding, no comparison theorem with algebraic cycles has been constructed. The chapter isolates what a framework proof of Hodge would have to look like: a rational lattice inside the (n,n) fixed-point set.

GRADE H · Historical commentary

Rigid by mirror symmetry

A point cloud on S³ (the Clifford torus fibration) rotates through R_N = N(N−1)/2 Lie planes and projects to your screen. In the 3D sector, R₃ = 3 and P₃ = 6: once A₀ pins the center, no residual reflection degree of freedom remains — the simply connected closed 3-manifold is rigid, a single sphere.

Honesty note

Perelman proved it in 2003 via Ricci flow with surgery; the prize was awarded in 2010 and declined. This module is a retro-reading: external confirmation of the framework's 3D rigidity claim, not a proof.
