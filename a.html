Chapter 1: The Zero-Point Division Crucible (\\(A_0\\)) and Scalar Seed Projection

At array position 0 of the universal sequence $A = [A_0, A_1, \dots, A_N]$, spatial extent is identically zero (\\(d = 0\\)). Slicing through the zero-point origin evaluates the indeterminate division $0/0$ as an unconstrained scalar seed $\lambda$:

$$\text{Axiom } A_0 \quad \equiv \quad A[0] : \quad 0 = 0, \quad \lim_{x \to 0} \frac{0}{x} = \lambda \in \mathbb{R}^N$$

Nodal symmetry breaking projects $\lambda$ outward along $N$ orthogonal cuts, dividing space into $H_N = 2^N$ hyper-octants and generating $R_N = \binom{N}{2}$ independent 2D rotation planes without background coordinate grids.

Chapter 3: Matrix Projection Math for 5D Hypercubes ($SO(5)$ Lie Algebra)

A 5D hypercube (penteract) consists of $2^5 = 32$ vertices defined by coordinate vectors $\mathbf{v} \in \{-1, 1\}^5$. Rotating a 5D vertex around 10 orthogonal rotation planes utilizes composite $SO(5)$ rotation matrices:

$$R_{5D}(\theta_1, \dots, \theta_{10}) = \prod_{1 \le a < b \le 5} R_{ab}(\theta_{ab})$$

Projecting from 5D $\to$ 4D $\to$ 3D $\to$ 2D perspective screen space utilizes successive focal distance normalization:

$$w_4 = \frac{d_5}{d_5 - v_5}, \quad w_3 = \frac{d_4}{d_4 - v_4 \cdot w_4}, \quad x_{\text{screen}} = c_x + v_1 \cdot w_3 \cdot w_4 \cdot S$$
