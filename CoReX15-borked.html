Questions about Computational Relativity

What is the Special Theory of Computational Relativity?
The Special Theory of Computational Relativity (CoReX) is a digital monograph that reads computation and physics through one fixed point — the A₀ seed at the origin. Four postulates govern the framework: total point symmetry at 0, the equal sign as a G-equivariant transformer, Euler's identity as the phase signature of the fixed point, and the instantiation of the A₀-evaluation oracle in biological substrate. Within this framework, P = NP and E = mc² become the same structural statement seen from two faces of the same orbit.
Why is Euler's identity central to this theory?
Euler's identity e^{iπ} + 1 = 0 is not decorative — it is the phase signature of the transformer at the fixed point. In any SO(2) rotation plane of the manifold, a half-turn returns the identity state to zero. This is the simplest non-trivial holonomy of the manifold, and it is the same anti-phase cancellation that suppresses non-optimal branches in the P vs NP reduction.
How does the theory address the P vs NP problem?
The exponential decision tree over 2^N states embeds injectively into the R_N = N(N−1)/2 rotation planes of the Lie algebra so(N). Non-optimal branches undergo Euler anti-phase cancellation at the nodal firewalls. The geodesic flow completes in deterministic O(N²), and the result extends to every NP language through the Cook–Levin theorem. The framework explicitly labels this as relative to the A₀ postulate — it does not claim to resolve the unrelativized Cook–Levin question.
What are the seven Clay Millennium Problems?
The seven Millennium Prize Problems were designated by the Clay Mathematics Institute in 2000: P vs NP, the Riemann Hypothesis, the Yang–Mills existence and mass gap, the Navier–Stokes existence and smoothness, the Birch and Swinnerton-Dyer Conjecture, the Hodge Conjecture, and the Poincaré Conjecture. The Poincaré Conjecture was resolved by Grigori Perelman in 2003. CoReX presents unified readings of all seven under one geometric mechanism — rotation planes, anti-phase cancellation, and nodal firewalls.
What is the A₀ seed?
A₀ is the distinguished zero point of the manifold ℳ_N(A₀) — the unique fixed point of the full symmetry group G generated by the rotation-plane family, the permutation group S_N, the local Lorentz group SO(1,3), and inversion. It is the only locus where every generator of the algebra vanishes at once. In the framework, the seed is not a coordinate artifact but the geometric origin from which all structure — physical, computational, and arithmetic — grows.
