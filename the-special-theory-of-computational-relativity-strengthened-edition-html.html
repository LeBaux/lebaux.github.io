Abstract

We present the revised dissertation of the special theory of computational relativity within an idealized N-dimensional manifold ℳN(A0) anchored by a foundational zero-point axiom (Axiom A0) at position 0 of the universal array A. Analogous to Einstein's special relativity, which posited the invariance of the speed of light c rather than deriving it, our framework postulates a free scalar seed λ rather than deriving it from an indeterminate form (the v100 limit was mathematically false). Within this universe we establish:

  1. A genuine Riemannian metric on ℳN \ {A0} with proved signature proved (Thm 3.1);
  2. Euler's identity as the expression of anti-phase equilibrium across A0 — a statement about our geometry, not a new theorem proved (Prop 3.2);
  3. Mass–energy equivalence as a Noether consequence of a boost-invariance postulate conditional (Thm 4.2);
  4. A restricted complexity theorem: SAT reduces to one oracle call with O(N²) encoding and decoding overhead proved (Thm 6.2), with the oracle explicitly isolated rather than hidden;
  5. A correct Landauer accounting lemma for time reversal proved (Lemma 5.1).

The chrono-structural time engine and the consciousness/HSAM chapters are retained as labeled Speculative Extensions. The universal claim that P = NP (Cook–Levin sense) follows from the geometry is dropped; the analysis of §6.1 shows precisely why the geometry cannot deliver it.

Chapter 1: Introduction and the special computational universe paradigm

1.1 The paradox of physical complexity limits

In standard complexity theory, NP problems are widely conjectured to be strictly separate from P. Evaluating multi-body path integrals across 2N hyper-octants forces exponential search O(2N), creating computational lag and reality friction.

1.2 The Einsteinian analogy — restated honestly

Einstein resolved the paradoxes of electrodynamics by constructing a theory bounded by a single invariant postulate: the constancy of c. Note carefully: he did not derive the postulate, and special relativity consequently did not prove anything about systems where the postulate fails.

Following that methodology, we define special computational relativity over a deterministic N-dimensional continuum ℳN(A0). What this edition claims — and all it claims — is that within the postulated universe, state representation over rotation planes is polynomial (Theorem 6.2), and the classical physical identities (Euler phase, E = mc² conditional on symmetry) are consistent geometric expressions of the model. What the analogy cannot deliver is a statement about the real-world P vs NP question, because the search step is not eliminated by the geometry (§6.3).

Chapter 2: Axiom A₀ at array position 0 — revised

2.1 The Seed Postulate axiom

In the universal axiom sequence A = [A0, A1, …, AN], the distinguished center is held at array index 0.

Axiom 2.1 (Seed Postulate)

There exists a smooth N-manifold ℳN with a distinguished point A0 ∈ ℳN and a free parameter λ ∈ ℝN. All spatial scale parameters of the theory are smooth functions of the components λ1, …, λN, which serve as global coordinates on ℳN \ {A0}.

Correction of v100.0.1. The original axiom stated limx→0 0/x = λ ∈ ℝN. This limit is identically 0, and the indeterminate form 0/0 is not a value in any case. The seed is now postulated, exactly as the constancy of c is postulated — the analogy is preserved, the false mathematics is removed.

2.2 Order invariance proved

Proposition 2.2

Any permutation σ ∈ SN of the secondary axioms induces a diffeomorphism Φσ of ℳN fixing A0; all intrinsic quantities (metric, curvature, the evaluation operator of Ch. 6) are Φσ-invariant.

Proof. Permuting coordinates is an orthogonal linear map on the λ-chart; intrinsic quantities are tensorial, hence invariant. ∎

2.3 The collapse operator ⊗ — withdrawn

The v100 claim that collisions of whole-number properties with fractional inverses "crystallize into fundamental physical constants" (δc, φc, 𝒪8) contained no definition of the operator ⊗: no domain, no codomain, no formula. Dimensional constants (c, ħ, G) cannot be the output of dimensionless combinatorics. The operator is withdrawn. Optionally, the observed fraction sequence (−1D→¼, −3D→⅙, −5D→⅛, i.e. κ(m) = 1/(2(|m|+2))) may be retained as a clearly labeled numerological conjecture in the notebooks; it is not part of the formal theory.

Chapter 3: Emergent geometry, SO(N) rotations, and Euler equilibrium

3.1 The metric tensor — now well-defined proved

Theorem 3.1

With λ1,…,λN the coordinates of Axiom 2.1, define

gij = Σk=1N ∂iλk ∂jλk

Then g is smooth and positive-definite on ℳN \ {A0} with signature (N, 0); hence (ℳN \ {A0}, g) is a Riemannian manifold.

Proof. On the λ-chart, ∂iλk = δik, so gij = δij: the Euclidean pullback, manifestly positive-definite. Degeneracy could occur only at A0, which the chart excludes. ∎

Correction of v100.0.1. Equation (2) of v100 added a term limx→0(0/x)·g(0)ij, which is identically zero, and asserted "Riemannian" without proving positive-definiteness. Both fixed: the dead term is removed, the signature is proved.

Slicing by N orthogonal nodal cuts through A0 yields HN = 2N hyper-octants and RN = C(N,2) independent rotation planes generating the Lie algebra 𝔰𝔬(N). These standard facts supply the state space of Chapter 6.

3.2 Euler's anti-phase equilibrium — reframed proved

Proposition 3.2

In any SO(2) rotation plane Π of TA₀ℳN, phase evolution is R(θ) = eiθ = cos θ + i sin θ. At θ = π, R(π) = −1, and the pair (identity state, anti-phase state) across the A0 mirror axis is expressed by

eiπ + 1 = 0

Epistemic status (corrected). This is standard Lie theory: complex phase is the generator of planar rotation. The v100 chapter titled this a "proof of Euler's identity" and a "boundary condition governing" the manifold — overstated. It is a statement about our geometry's expression, carrying no computational or physical consequence. Retained with corrected status.

Chapter 4: Mass–energy equivalence — decirculated conditional

Correction of v100.0.1. The v100 derivation defined ρm ≡ u/c² (Eq. 6) and then integrated to obtain E = mc² (Eq. 8). Substituting a definition into its own integral is a tautology: the conclusion was contained in the premise, and no physics was derived. The circular route is replaced by the symmetry route below.
Postulate 4.1 (Lagrangian and boost invariance)

Field dynamics on ℳN are governed by a Lagrangian density ℒ(φ, ∂φ) invariant under rotations and boosts acting on the (ℳN, time) product, i.e. under a subgroup isomorphic to the Lorentz group SO(1,3).

Theorem 4.2 (conditional E = mc²)

Assuming Postulate 4.1, Noether's theorem yields a conserved energy–momentum current satisfying

E² = |p|²c² + m²c⁴

and for the field configuration at rest over a region Ω centered at A0:

Etotal = ∭Ω u(r, t) d³r = mc²,  m := ∭Ω ρm d³r

Status. The conclusion now follows from a symmetry postulate, following Einstein's logic of the inertia of energy and its modern Noether formulation. Open work: exhibiting the explicit ℒ. Until then the result is conditional, and the monograph does not claim to derive relativity from computation.

Chapter 5 (Speculative Extensions, Part I): Landauer accounting and the time reversal engine

Status of this chapter: speculative. The orbital gear-mesh geometry (δ tier: 4 lobes, 2 nodes; φ tier: 6 lobes, 3 nodes; 𝒪₈ tier: 8 lobes, 4 nodes) is retained as labeled analogy: in quantum mechanics, orbital lobes do not transmit mechanical torque, and no dynamical model is given here. The v100 wavefunction Ψmesh (Eq. 9), which multiplied a torque sum, a firewall step-function, and a Boltzmann factor into one expression with no defined probability space, is withdrawn. What survives is extracted as a formal lemma:

Lemma 5.1 (Landauer accounting under time reversal) proved

Let 𝒯rev act on a state space of n bits. The minimum heat dissipated is

ΔE ≥ nerased · kBT ln 2

where nerased is the number of bits not recoverable after reversal. If 𝒯rev is logically reversible (bijective), then nerased = 0 and no Landauer heat is required (Landauer 1961; Bennett 1973).

Corollary + flagged tension. The claim that "nodal firewalls absorb Landauer heat" means formally that 𝒯rev must carry an explicit erasure log. Otherwise reversal is thermodynamically free only if logically reversible — which forbids the trajectory-collapsing behavior the v100 text also assumed. These two assumptions are in tension; this is flagged, not resolved. Nodal surfaces |ψnode| = 0 remain an evocative metaphor for the isolation of trajectory classes, with no formal status in this edition.

Chapter 6: Complexity — the restricted theorem

Withdrawal of Theorem 6.1 (v100). The v100 proof of P = NP failed at its step 4: "zero-lag determinism" (𝒞lag ≡ 1, 𝒯solve = 0) was asserted, not derived, and a model in which solving is free is not a model of computation — it is equivalent to assuming a free SAT oracle. Additionally, no reduction from any NP-complete problem was ever constructed. This chapter replaces the claim with what the mathematics actually supports.

6.1 Encoding is not the barrier — verified verified

Observation 6.1

For SAT on N variables, the space of rotation-plane configurations (RN = C(N,2) planes, binary angle quantization) has size 2C(N,2) ≥ 2N for all N ≥ 3. An injective encoding of truth assignments into plane configurations exists trivially.

Verification. Checked numerically for N = 4, 8, 16, 32, 64: address spaces of 6, 28, 120, 496, 2016 bits always exceed the N-bit assignment space. The information-theoretic obstruction vanishes. What remains is the search problem — locating the satisfying configuration — which is precisely the P vs NP core and cannot be eliminated by geometry. ∎

6.2 The Restricted Complexity Theorem proved

Theorem 6.2

Fix ℳN(A0) with an oracle A₀-SAT that, in one unit call, returns a satisfying rotation-plane configuration if one exists. Then SAT on N variables is solvable with: (i) O(N²) operations to encode the instance as constraints over the RN rotation planes; (ii) one oracle call; (iii) O(N²) operations to decode and verify. Total non-oracle cost: O(N²).

Proof. Each CNF clause constrains ≤ 3 variables; each variable maps injectively to a dedicated plane (Observation 6.1 gives ample address space); compilation is linear in clauses, hence O(N²); decoding reads one plane angle, O(1) per variable; verification is the standard linear-time SAT check. ∎

6.3 Where the barrier now lives claim withdrawn

Theorem 6.2 does not prove P = NP. It proves SAT reduces to one oracle call with polynomial overhead. The oracle — "the manifold returns the satisfying configuration" — is exactly the v100 assumption 𝒯solve = 0, now isolated as a single explicit postulate instead of hidden inside "zero-lag." This is the well-known situation of P = NP relative to a SAT oracle: trivially true, carrying no information about the unrelativized question. What the geometry genuinely contributes is the state-space structure and the polynomial encoding/decoding overhead — a modest but real result about representation.

6.4 The constructive program forward

To de-relativize Theorem 6.2 one must replace the oracle with an algorithm. Candidate routes: (a) interior-point or ellipsoid methods over the angle space — clause satisfaction is non-convex, no known exact relaxation (mirroring the known hardness of algebraic relaxations of SAT); (b) symmetry reduction via Prop 2.2 — quotienting by SN saves at most a factorial factor, insufficient against 2N. Conclusion: within this framework the barrier is exactly the standard P vs NP barrier; the framework neither circumvents nor deepens it, and this is stated plainly.

Chapter 7 (Speculative Extensions, Part II): Consciousness and HSAM

Status: speculative. The v100 definition 𝒞(t⃗) = |d²t⃗/dτ²| = Q(t⃗) requires, to be non-vacuous: (1) t⃗ not parallel to its own parameter — otherwise d²t⃗/dτ² ≡ 0 identically and the "consciousness function" is identically zero (the vacuity check v100 never ran); (2) a defined interpretation of τ; (3) one falsifiable prediction, e.g. a claimed correlation of |d²t⃗/dτ²| with a measurable neural quantity. None are supplied. The function is presented as a definition in search of a dynamical model, not a result.

HSAM. Highly Superior Autobiographical Memory is an empirically studied, rare memory phenotype. "Lossless temporal indexing" is retained as metaphor; a minimal formalization would define lossless indexing as an isometric embedding of the subjective time axis into memory state space — a testable information-theoretic claim, offered as future work.
